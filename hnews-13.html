BODIES DISCOVERED AT LEGENDS MOTEL

Havenfall Chronicles
Share:

BODIES DISCOVERED

HAVENFALL — Authorities have opened an investigation into the Legends Motel following the discovery of several bodies inside one of the motel's rooms earlier this week.

Police were called to the establishment shortly after midnight following reports of an unusual odor coming from one of the rooms. Officers entered the room and discovered multiple deceased individuals inside.

The identities of the victims have not yet been released.

According to authorities, there were no immediate signs of a break-in, although investigators have not ruled out foul play. The motel has since been temporarily closed while forensic teams examine the property.

Residents living near the motel described the discovery as disturbing, particularly given the motel's reputation for attracting travelers and individuals looking for inexpensive accommodation.

One former employee, who requested anonymity, claimed that the room involved had been occupied several times over the past few weeks.

"People came and went. Nobody really paid attention," the former employee said.

Authorities have urged members of the public not to speculate about the identities of the victims or the circumstances surrounding their deaths while the investigation remains ongoing.

However, several details surrounding the case remain unclear.

Investigators have not disclosed how long the bodies had been inside the room, nor have they explained why nobody reported anything unusual before the discovery.

Residents have also reported seeing a young woman around the motel in the days preceding the incident.

Authorities have not confirmed whether the woman is connected to the investigation.

For now, the Legends Motel remains closed.

The investigation is ongoing.
