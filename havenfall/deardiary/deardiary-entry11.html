Dear Diary,


I ended up not going with the truck idea.

It was absolutely stupid.

It needed a lot of work, and that was going to give me a lot of problems.

The good thing is that I got two days without work, so that's better than anything. Tomorrow I'm not going to work either.

The delivery truck will be here to move the things I need to my storage rooms in Helen's Field.

Only the important things.

Which isn't a problem.

Only the driver would know about it.

I guess I'm not feeling that motivated today.

It's because Melissa doesn't seem okay.

I don't know what's wrong with her.

I'm concerned.

Lexie is boring.

Yeah, I did talk with her. She told me she lives alone, which is good because then I can make things work well over the next two days.

The storage rooms aren't mine.

I'm just lucky enough to have the key to one of them.

I weirdly don't feel like writing today.

This Melissa thing is concerning me.

Maybe tomorrow will be better.

I hope so.
