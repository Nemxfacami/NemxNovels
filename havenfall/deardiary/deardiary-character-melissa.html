Basic Profile

Age : 26

Role : The woman at the center of the diary; the one being watched



Personality

Traits

  • Warm, affectionate, and quick to smile; makes people feel cared for.
  • Open-hearted. Once she trusts someone, she talks freely about her mother, her past, and the people who hurt her.
  • Playful and teasing, such as stealing food from a friend's plate and laughing about it.
  • Wild and full of life in social settings; drinks and dances without holding back.
  • Needs quiet and avoids big lunch groups, preferring one-on-one company.
  • Thoughtful about her loyalties; stops speaking to people who wrong her and can explain exactly why.
  • Gentle by nature. Believes she would never hurt anyone, and spends real effort explaining why.


Flaws

  • Reads attention as affection and kindness as proof of good intentions
  • Says “I'm fine” when she isn't, and hides what bothers her
  • Overshares with anyone who seems to listen
  • Careless with words said in anger, since she never imagines anyone taking them literally


Motivations

  • Being understood by someone without having to explain herself
  • Feeling safe, with someone she can rely on
  • Putting the people who hurt her behind her


Hates

  • Being late
  • Onions
  • Getting wet in the rain, even though she loves the rain
  • Men who stare, crowd, or get too close without asking


Strengths

  • Generous with her time, her laughter, and her warmth
  • Emotionally open and easy to talk to
  • Sentimental about small gestures; remembers the people who remember her
  • Loyal to those who earn it


Fears

  • Being hurt again by someone she trusted
  • Being watched or followed — a fear she has not yet put into words
  • Being left alone with her thoughts
  • That her anger might one day become something real


Relationships

  • Lexie Narlim — her coworker and closest confidant; she says he makes her feel safe, though she knows less about him than he knows about her
  • Dave — her coworker, friendly and kind, and spending more and more time around her
  • Mickey — her next-door neighbor, whose staring made her uncomfortable
  • Her mother — a subject she opens up about only to those she trusts


Hobbies

  • Eating lunch quietly with one person instead of a crowd
  • Walking in the rain (with an umbrella)
  • Parties and dancing
  • Ice cream, especially her favorite flavor


Backstory

Melissa works on floor -16, where she is known as warm, easy to talk to, and a little guarded about who she lets sit beside her. She has cut off three people in her life, and a man from her past still lingers in her thoughts. Her bond with her mother runs deep.
