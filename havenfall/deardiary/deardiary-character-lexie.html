Backstory

Lexie Narlim is a 28-year-old woman who works at a restaurant and lives a relatively ordinary life. Her job has made her comfortable dealing with different kinds of people, from friendly regulars to difficult customers, and has taught her to stay patient when things become stressful. Outside of work, Lexie maintains a small circle of friends, including Melissa, with whom she has developed a comfortable friendship. She is not someone who goes looking for trouble or mystery; she is simply trying to work, maintain her friendships, and get through everyday life.



Trivia

Lexie is one of the more ordinary people surrounding Melissa. Unlike some of the stranger characters in the story, there is currently nothing particularly unusual about her. Her restaurant job means she spends much of her day reading people's moods and dealing with different personalities. She is 28 years old and is one of Melissa's friends.
