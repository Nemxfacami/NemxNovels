Basic Profile

Age : 28

Affiliation : Office worker; works with “subjects”

Status : Alive

Role : The diary writer; the one doing the watching



Personality

Traits

  • Quiet, calm, and soft-spoken; the kind of person people forget to be careful around.
  • Extremely observant. Notices walking patterns, tired eyes, and the way someone looks at their phone.
  • Attentive and caring on the surface: offers his umbrella, remembers favorite flavors, sits with people who are sad.
  • A patient listener who lets others talk until they reveal themselves.
  • Predictable in habits, with the same seat and the same order.
  • Self-aware about his loneliness and willing to admit it, but only to his diary.
  • Believes that kindness is simple and that most people are just not paying attention.


Flaws

  • Treats attention as ownership
  • Cannot stand jealousy, rivals, or anyone who gets close to what he considers his
  • Believes a connection does not need to be genuine to count
  • Uses his work techniques on people who trust him
  • Turns loneliness into obsession


Motivations

  • Ending his loneliness
  • Being known as deeply as he knows others
  • Keeping Melissa safe, and close, from everyone else


Hates

  • Loneliness and empty cafés
  • Cheating and people who “play around with their hearts”
  • Men who stare at Melissa
  • Being ignored when he has been paying attention


Strengths

  • Exceptional memory for small personal details
  • Reads people quickly and adjusts what he says to them
  • Composed under pressure
  • Makes others feel seen, safe, and understood almost instantly


Fears

  • Being alone
  • Losing Melissa to someone else
  • Being truly known by someone — the thing he says he wants most
  • Nightmares, which he mentions only in passing


Relationships

  • Melissa Chandretta — his coworker and the center of his world; he calls her his love, and she thinks he is the kindest person she knows
  • Dave — a coworker he likes, but one whose growing closeness to Melissa troubles him
  • Mickey — Melissa's neighbor, whom he despised on sight
  • His diary — the only place he tells the whole truth


Hobbies

  • Writing in his diary every night
  • Having lunch with Melissa
  • Quietly noticing the people around him
  • Testing small statements on people to see how they respond


Backstory

Lexie works in the same building as Melissa, in a job where he learns to read subjects without asking them anything directly. To everyone else he is polite, quiet, and a little strange. He keeps a diary that began as an outlet for his loneliness. Over time it begins to answer him, or so he feels, and it keeps giving him ideas. By the time anyone notices how closely he watches, he has already decided what Melissa will become to him █████ ████ █████.
