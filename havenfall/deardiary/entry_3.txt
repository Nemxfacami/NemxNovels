Dear Diary,<br><br><br>
Melissa has been taking leave from work.<br><br>
I didn't expect that to happen. I wonder why she did that to me. She should have at least told me she wouldn't be at work instead of me having to hear it from Dave.<br><br>
I like Dave. He's kind and friendly to talk to.<br><br>
So I decided to ask for a day off too. I'm going home first, then I'll go see Melissa.<br><br>
I'm really hoping I get to find the café still open today. Unlike last time, when I had to make it myself because no one was there.<br><br>
It was lonely.<br><br>
I hate loneliness.<br><br>
It makes me ask myself what would end it.<br><br>
But then I remember that humans need connection, and I'm a human. The good thing is, it doesn't really say that the connection has to be genuine.<br><br>
So at least I can find ways to make someone connect with me.<br><br>
Right?<br><br>
I guess it's a yes.<br><br>
I think this is a good start.<br><br>
Melissa will be mine.<br><br>
I'll confess to her. Maybe then we can connect more, because this loneliness makes me sick.<br><br>
Something strange happened while I was writing this.<br><br>
I feel like the diary is talking to me.<br><br>
Not literally, obviously.<br><br>
But it keeps giving me great ideas.<br><br>
Now I understand why females have them.<br><br>
I should go.<br><br>
I have to pick up some rope and dark tape.<br><br>
