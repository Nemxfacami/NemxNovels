Dear Diary,<br><br><br>
Today was a wonderful day.<br><br>
Melissa smiled at me.<br><br>
A lot.<br><br>
I like it when she smiles.<br><br>
I think I might be the reason for some of them.<br><br>
Maybe. Hopefully.<br><br>
We had lunch together today. She doesn't seem to be into having lunch with groups of people, maybe she just loves peace or doesn't like talking a lot.<br><br>
She said I am becoming predictable.<br><br>
I asked her what that meant.<br><br>
She said I always sit in the same place, order the same thing, and somehow always know when she is about to steal food from my plate.<br><br>
I told her that was because she always steals food from my plate.<br><br>
She laughed.<br><br>
Then she stole my chicken.<br><br>
I let her.<br><br>
It was a small piece anyway.<br><br>
I bought her ice cream afterward.<br><br>
She asked why.<br><br>
I told her because she looked tired.<br><br>
She said she wasn't tired.<br><br>
She was.<br><br>
Her eyes get smaller when she is tired.<br><br>
She also starts rubbing her thumb against the side of her hand.<br><br>
She doesn't know she does that.<br><br>
But I do.<br><br>
I notice a lot of things about Melissa.<br><br>
The way she walks when she is happy.<br><br>
The way she walks when she is angry.<br><br>
The way she says "I'm fine" when she isn't fine.<br><br>
The way she looks at her phone when she is waiting for someone to message her.<br><br>
The way she smiles before reading a message she already knows she wants to read.<br><br>
I wonder if she notices things about me.<br><br>
I wonder if she notices my loneliness.<br><br>
How empty I can be at times.<br><br>
I asked her.<br><br>
She said I am quiet.<br><br>
That is true.<br><br>
Then she said I am strange.<br><br>
That is also true.<br><br>
Then she said I am kind.<br><br>
I liked that one.<br><br>
I asked her why.<br><br>
She said because I always notice when she needs something.<br><br>
I didn't tell her that I notice everything.<br><br>
That would sound strange, but I wish I could tell her so she too can notice everything about me.<br><br>
So she can know me as much as I know her.<br><br>
Instead I told her I just pay attention.<br><br>
She said:<br><br>
"That's what makes you different."<br><br>
I don't think I'm different. I'm just being myself. It helps me to notice things so I can learn and understand others.<br><br>
I just don't understand why people don't pay attention.<br><br>
If someone is cold, give them your jacket.<br><br>
If someone is hungry, give them food.<br><br>
If someone is sad, sit with them.<br><br>
It's not difficult.<br><br>
Melissa had forgotten her umbrella today.<br><br>
It was raining when we left work.<br><br>
I gave her mine.<br><br>
She asked what I was going to use.<br><br>
I told her I would run.<br><br>
She laughed and said I would get sick.<br><br>
So she held the umbrella over both of us.<br><br>
We walked very close.<br><br>
Our shoulders touched.<br><br>
I liked that. She felt warm and cozy.<br><br>
I felt the need to hug her.<br><br>
I wonder if she would hug me back.<br><br>
She didn't move away.<br><br>
I liked that even more.<br><br>
Maybe she likes me.<br><br>
I think she does.<br><br>
She told me I make her feel safe.<br><br>
That made me happy.<br><br>
I want Melissa to always feel safe around me.<br><br>
There are many things in the world that can hurt people.<br><br>
Bad people.<br><br>
Bad decisions.<br><br>
Bad luck.<br><br>
Mickey.<br><br>
Dave.<br><br>
Other things.<br><br>
I don't like those things.<br><br>
But I can handle them.<br><br>
Melissa doesn't have to worry about them.<br><br>
She can just be Melissa.<br><br>
She told me something before she left.<br><br>
She said she likes that I remember small things.<br><br>
I remembered her favorite ice cream.<br><br>
I remembered she doesn't like onions.<br><br>
I remembered she hates being late.<br><br>
I remembered she likes the rain but hates getting wet.<br><br>
She asked how I remember all of that.<br><br>
I told her:<br><br>
"I listen."<br><br>
She smiled.<br><br>
Then she hugged me.<br><br>
It lasted four seconds.<br><br>
I counted.<br><br>
I don't know why I counted.<br><br>
I just did.<br><br>
Four seconds is a long time.<br><br>
I think tomorrow I'll buy her the ice cream she likes again.<br><br>
Maybe I'll get two.<br><br>
One for her.<br><br>
One for me.<br><br>
That way she won't have to steal mine.<br><br>
Although I don't really mind when she does.<br><br>
Goodnight, Diary.<br><br>
Melissa says I'm kind.<br><br>
I think I like being kind. It's nice to be kind.<br><br>
