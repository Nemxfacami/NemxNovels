Dear Diary,


Today I was with Melissa in the same room, but we didn't talk because that bastard brought her in for some evaluation.

Didn't she grasp the situation from seeing what he's been doing to me?

He took her there without answering when he said she should rate my conversation with him from the video we watched.

I don't know if she was trying to mess with me or something.

That dumb bitch. Just because it's not happening to her, she sat there and didn't say anything at all.

Does she even have any idea how that bastard has punished me?

I don't understand Melissa anymore.

Maybe it's the snake's venom in my veins. I feel sick.

In the end, Zee gave Melissa a choice of tools to beat me with.

I thought today I would receive a reward, but it was completely different from what I thought.

It stings so much.

That dumb bitch chose something so thin. It hurts more because it digs in deeper.

I can't be punished tomorrow.

I must receive a reward at all costs.

I'm already prepared to throw away my pride for that.

What happened today was because Melissa flustered me.

Even so, what should I do to redeem myself?
