# Havenfall
Havenfall — A psychological horror and mystery novel.


**Havenfall** is a city that's an existential psychological horror novel , a place where reality feels unstable and every answer uncovers a deeper mystery. The story follows characters drawn into a chain of strange events involving disappearances, hidden organizations, disturbing rituals, and the growing fear that Havenfall itself is changing them.

This repository contains the source files for the **Havenfall** project, including chapters, worldbuilding notes, and related assets used for publication on **NemxNovels**.

## Themes

* Psychological horror
* Mystery and suspense
* Existential dread
* Character-driven storytelling
* Hidden organizations and rituals
* Reality, memory, and identity

## Status

The novel is currently in active development, with chapters being edited and expanded for publication.
