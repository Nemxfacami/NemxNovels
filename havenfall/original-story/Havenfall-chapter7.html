Elizabeth smiled sadly.

"I know being sorry can't erase what you experienced."

Cyrus smiled slightly.

"Haha..."

He looked away.

"That's true, my love."

Elizabeth smiled.

"There you go."

"What?"

"Being dramatic."

Cyrus laughed softly.

"And you're still annoying."

"And you're still stuck with me."

They stayed there together as the sun disappeared beyond the horizon.

For a little while...

the world was quiet.

And that was enough.
