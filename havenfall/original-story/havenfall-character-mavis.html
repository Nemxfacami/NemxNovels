Backstory

Mavis Mathens the Fourth is part of the Mathens family and appears comfortable within the family's wealthy and hierarchical environment.

Her introduction presents her as one of the lighter personalities in the family.

At the Mathens estate, she immediately welcomes Billie and draws her into the family gathering.

She treats Billie casually rather than making her feel like an outsider.

However, the relationship becomes more complicated later.

Mavis takes Billie upstairs to a room filled with pale green smoke and tells Matt that she only wanted Billie to relax.

Billie becomes heavily affected by the substance.

Matt eventually discovers her incapacitated and removes her from the situation.

This moment reveals that Mavis is not simply the harmless comic-relief member of the Mathens family.

Her playful personality can coexist with very questionable behavior.

The story has not yet explained what the green smoke was, why Mavis had it, or whether she understood its effects.

  • She also becomes one of the first people within the family to directly alter Billie's physical state.
  • Her behavior creates an important contradiction between hospitality and control.

  • She doesn't currently appear to be motivated by the larger Havenfall mystery in the way Victor, Martin, or Belladonna might be.
  • Her exact knowledge of the supernatural events remains unknown.
  • The story has not established whether the green smoke was merely recreational, medicinal, supernatural, or something else.
  • Her surname's “the Fourth” suggests a family naming tradition or generational numbering that could become relevant to the Mathens family structure later.
  • Mavis currently functions as an interesting contrast within the Mathens family: she makes the family feel welcoming while simultaneously demonstrating that being welcomed into the family doesn't necessarily mean being safe.
