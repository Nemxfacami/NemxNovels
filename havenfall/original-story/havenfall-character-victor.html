Basic Profile

Age : 54

Affiliation : Mathens Family

Status : Alive

Residence : Victor-Kenethe Estate

Family : Andrew Mathens, Matt Mathens, Martin Mathens, and other Mathens family members

Role : Mathens family patriarch / senior authority; connected to Martin's discoveries and the mysterious book



Personality

Traits

  • Authoritative — Victor's presence establishes immediate hierarchy.
  • Controlled — He rarely reacts impulsively.
  • Experienced — His behavior suggests someone accustomed to dealing with complicated situations.
  • Observant — He notices important details without needing them explained.
  • Private — Victor keeps his knowledge and emotions close to himself.
  • Decisive — Once he understands a situation, he gives instructions rather than debating endlessly.
  • Calm — Even the discovery of strange information doesn't produce an obvious emotional outburst.
  • Formal — His interactions carry the weight of family hierarchy and tradition.
  • Self-contained — He doesn't seem dependent on other people's approval.
  • Investigative — He is willing to examine Martin's material personally.
  • Protective of family interests — His immediate concern is understanding what has happened and what the family is dealing with.
  • Difficult to read — His restrained behavior makes his true thoughts difficult to determine.


Flaws

  • Secretive — Victor clearly knows more than he openly reveals.
  • Emotionally restrained — His controlled nature makes it difficult for others to know what he actually feels.
  • Hierarchical — He naturally expects family members to follow established authority.
  • Information control — He appears to decide carefully who deserves to know what.
  • Potentially dismissive of personal boundaries — His authority allows him to make decisions for others without lengthy explanations.
  • Silence can create suspicion — His refusal to answer Andrew's question about Martin makes his knowledge appear more significant.
  • Prioritizes larger family concerns — Individual emotions can become secondary to the survival or interests of the Mathens family.


Motivations

  • Protect the Mathens family.
  • Understand what Martin discovered.
  • Determine the significance of the mysterious book.
  • Understand the threat surrounding Martin's death.
  • Maintain family authority and stability.
  • Protect information that could endanger the family.
  • Determine what Andrew and Matt have uncovered.
  • Decide how the family should respond to the discoveries in Martin's bunker.
  • His exact personal motivation regarding the supernatural mystery is not yet established.


Hates

  • Not explicitly established, but his behavior suggests he has little tolerance for:
  • Threats against the Mathens family.
  • Disorder within the family.
  • Important information being mishandled.
  • People acting without authority.
  • Situations he cannot control or understand.
  • Family members bringing dangerous problems into the estate without preparation.
  • These are inferred from his behavior rather than directly stated.


Strengths

  • Authority — His position gives him substantial influence within the Mathens family.
  • Composure — He remains controlled when confronted with disturbing information.
  • Experience — He appears accustomed to serious family matters and dangerous situations.
  • Decision-making — Gives clear instructions when circumstances require action.
  • Information management — Understands the importance of keeping sensitive knowledge contained.
  • Observation — Immediately recognizes that Martin's book is significant.
  • Discipline — Maintains his composure rather than allowing surprise to dictate his behavior.
  • Leadership — Commands obedience without needing to constantly demonstrate force.
  • Strategic thinking — Appears to think beyond the immediate situation.
  • Family influence — Has enough authority to direct Matt and Andrew without prolonged arguments.


Fears

  • Victor's personal fears have not been revealed, but his behavior suggests possible concerns about:
  • The threat surrounding Martin's discoveries.
  • The Mathens family becoming exposed to something it cannot control.
  • Information from Martin's research falling into the wrong hands.
  • The mysterious book revealing something dangerous.
  • Whatever Martin discovered reaching the wider world.
  • Internal instability following Martin's death.
  • One particularly interesting possibility is that Victor may already understand the danger represented by the book, which could explain his immediate reaction to seeing it. That remains unconfirmed.


Relationships

  • Andrew Mathens — Son. Andrew has a much more relaxed relationship with Victor than Matt does. Andrew is willing to joke, ask questions, and bring unexpected discoveries directly to his father. Victor nevertheless remains the authority figure. When Andrew brings him Martin's mysterious book, Victor immediately takes control of the situation and asks him to explain everything.
  • Matt Mathens — Son. Victor has a more formal relationship with Matt. Matt is already accustomed to authority and hierarchy, so their interactions are controlled rather than openly emotional. Victor immediately notices the situation surrounding Billie and orders Matt to take her to Dr. Zen. This demonstrates that Victor can override Matt's personal decisions when necessary.
  • Martin Mathens — Son / deceased. Martin is possibly the most important relationship for Victor's current storyline. Martin's death leads Andrew and Matt to investigate his hidden facility. Inside the bunker, they discover evidence that Martin had been investigating something enormous involving Havenfall, the Hole of Edenus, the Edenus Foundation, and other unexplained phenomena. When Andrew asks Victor “Martin… Did he ever tell you about this?” Victor stops moving but does not answer. That silence is significant. It doesn't prove Victor knew about Martin's research, but it establishes that something about the question affects him.
  • Billie Kagon — Outsider connected to Matt. Victor initially assumes Billie is Matt's pregnant girlfriend when he sees her arrive. The assumption is incorrect, but the scene demonstrates how quickly Victor evaluates the situation in front of him. Later, he allows her to receive medical treatment at his estate rather than simply sending her away.
  • Mathens Family — Victor represents the highest visible level of Mathens family authority in the story so far. Where Belladonna represents family authority within meetings and Andrew represents social influence, Victor represents the patriarchal structure at the top of the family.


Hobbies

  • Sitting by the fireplace.
  • Drinking wine.
  • Personally examining important objects.
  • Managing family affairs.
  • Receiving information from his sons.
  • Making decisions regarding family members.
  • He appears to value privacy, controlled environments, and direct examination of important information.


Backstory

Victor's personal history has not yet been revealed.

What is established is that he is a senior member and patriarch of the Mathens family.

His estate functions as a controlled environment with security and even a medical wing, suggesting the family's resources and influence extend considerably beyond ordinary wealth.

His significance increases dramatically when Andrew and Matt discover Martin's underground research facility.

The facility contains:

• Maps of Havenfall.
• Photographs of the Hole of Edenus.
• Edenus Foundation blueprints.
• Names and timelines.
• Symbols.
• Martin's notes.
• Books.
• Tapes.
• Journals.
• An unidentified book written in an unrecognized language.

Andrew and Matt bring the book to Victor.

Victor immediately recognizes that the object is important and handles it carefully.

When Andrew asks whether Martin ever told him about the research, Victor doesn't answer.

Instead, he stops.

That moment creates an unresolved connection between Victor, Martin, and whatever Martin discovered.

Victor's true level of knowledge remains unknown.



Trivia

  • Victor's first major scene establishes him as someone who expects information to come to him in an organized manner.
  • He is introduced as “Father” within the Mathens family hierarchy.
  • He immediately recognizes the mysterious book from Martin's bunker.
  • Andrew's question about Martin produces one of Victor's most significant reactions: silence.
  • He prefers personally examining important objects rather than simply accepting someone else's description.
  • His estate contains a medical facility, demonstrating the family's considerable resources.
  • Victor doesn't appear easily shocked by unusual circumstances.
  • He is more restrained than Andrew, more institutional than Matt, and less theatrically commanding than Belladonna.
  • His power comes largely from position, experience, and information.
  • The story deliberately gives him very few emotional tells, making his smallest reactions more important.
  • Victor's relationship with Martin is currently one of the biggest unanswered Mathens-family questions.
  • He may know something about Martin's investigation, but the chapters have not confirmed exactly what he knew or when he knew it.
  • His silence is therefore more important than any exposition could have been.
  • The mysterious book creates a possible connection between the Mathens family and the supernatural events surrounding Havenfall.
  • Victor currently occupies an interesting position in the story: he may be investigating the mystery, protecting the family from it, or already know far more about it than Andrew and Matt realize.
  • Which of those possibilities is true has deliberately not been answered yet.
