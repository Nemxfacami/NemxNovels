Basic Profile

Age : 35

Affiliation : Edenus Foundation

Status : Alive

Residence : Edenus Foundation, Havenfall

Family : Connected to the Stiles family

Role : Foundation member / field operative / collector; responsible for gathering the “unclean” and assisting Alexander with the Foundation's preparations



Personality

Traits

  • Quiet — Aleister doesn't waste words and generally communicates directly.
  • Practical — His behavior suggests someone accustomed to completing tasks rather than discussing them.
  • Obedient — He appears comfortable carrying out Alexander's instructions.
  • Composed — He doesn't display much emotional reaction to unusual circumstances.
  • Purpose-driven — His actions seem connected to a larger objective rather than personal entertainment.
  • Observant — He notices what is happening around the Foundation and reports back to Alexander.
  • Reserved — Unlike Emily or Ethan, he doesn't express himself openly.
  • Capable — He handles Foundation responsibilities without needing constant supervision.
  • Secretive — Much of what he actually knows remains hidden.
  • Loyal — His actions indicate considerable loyalty to Alexander and the Foundation.
  • Comfortable with the abnormal — His behavior suggests that strange events are not enough to make him abandon his responsibilities.
  • Serious when necessary — Although the Stiles family has moments of ordinary humor, Aleister's role places him closer to the Foundation's darker machinery.


Flaws

  • Blind obedience — His willingness to follow Foundation instructions may prevent him from questioning their consequences.
  • Withholds information — He doesn't explain what he knows about the people or events surrounding the Foundation.
  • Prioritizes duty over individuals — His responsibilities appear more important than the immediate feelings or safety of others.
  • Morally difficult position — If “the unclean” refers to people being gathered for the Foundation's purposes, his participation makes him part of a disturbing process.
  • Emotionally restrained — His limited expression makes it difficult to know what he actually thinks about the Foundation's actions.
  • Accepts the Foundation's worldview — He operates within its terminology and structure rather than challenging it.
  • Potentially too trusting of authority — Alexander's position appears sufficient for Aleister to act without demanding extensive explanations.


Motivations

  • Serve the Edenus Foundation.
  • Carry out Alexander's instructions.
  • Gather the people Alexander refers to as “the unclean.”
  • Prepare the Foundation for the approaching event.
  • Support whatever process surrounds the Chosen One.
  • Maintain the Foundation's order.
  • Fulfill his assigned responsibilities.
  • His personal motivation beyond his Foundation duties is not yet established.


Hates

  • Not explicitly established. There is currently no clear scene showing Aleister expressing hatred toward a person, group, or concept, but his behavior does suggest opposition to:
  • Disobedience to Foundation authority.
  • Interference with Foundation preparations.
  • Anything that prevents him from completing his assigned tasks.
  • These are inferences from his actions rather than confirmed personal beliefs.


Strengths

  • Reliability — Alexander appears comfortable depending on him.
  • Discipline — He completes tasks without unnecessary hesitation.
  • Composure — Strange situations don't easily disrupt him.
  • Practicality — Focuses on what needs to be done.
  • Loyalty — Strong connection to the Foundation's hierarchy.
  • Discretion — Doesn't reveal information unnecessarily.
  • Ability to operate independently — Alexander doesn't appear to need to supervise his every action.
  • Familiarity with the Foundation — He understands its people, routines, and objectives.
  • Physical capability — His work involving gathering people and working around the Foundation suggests practical competence.


Fears

  • Aleister's fears have not been directly revealed. There is no clear evidence that he fears the supernatural phenomena surrounding Havenfall. In fact, his calm behavior suggests that he may have become accustomed to things that would frighten ordinary people.
  • Possible concerns suggested by the story include:
  • The Foundation failing to complete its preparations.
  • Alexander's vision not coming to pass.
  • Losing control of the people being gathered.
  • Something interfering with the Chosen One's development.
  • None of these are confirmed as personal fears.


Relationships

  • Alexander Stiles — Authority / Foundation leader. Alexander appears to be Aleister's superior within the Foundation. Their relationship is primarily functional. Aleister brings information to Alexander, while Alexander provides the larger context and purpose behind their work. The clearest example is Aleister's statement “I've gathered the unclean.” Alexander responds as though this is part of an ongoing plan. This suggests Aleister is not merely a passive member of the Foundation. He is actively helping execute its plans.
  • Emily Stiles — Foundation member / Chosen One. Aleister is connected to Emily through the Foundation. Unlike Alexander, however, the story has not yet established a personal emotional relationship between them. His role appears to be part of the machinery surrounding Emily's transformation.
  • Ethan Stiles — Family connection. Aleister is connected to the Stiles household, although the exact nature of his relationship with Ethan has not yet been deeply explored.
  • Levi Stiles — Family connection. Likewise, his exact relationship with Levi has not yet been developed.
  • Edenus Foundation — Organization. The Foundation is currently Aleister's most important established relationship. He appears to function as someone who operates within the Foundation's system rather than merely believing in it.


Hobbies

  • No genuine hobbies have been established. His known activities are primarily connected to:
  • Foundation work.
  • Gathering people.
  • Assisting Alexander.
  • Moving through the Foundation grounds.
  • Carrying out assigned responsibilities.


Backstory

Aleister's personal history remains largely unknown.

He is introduced as part of the Stiles family environment surrounding the Edenus Foundation.

During the Foundation's activities, he tells Alexander:

“I've gathered the unclean.”

The statement is important because it establishes that Aleister has an active role in whatever the Foundation is preparing.

Later, when Alexander explains the vision and the Chosen One to Anthony, Aleister's earlier actions gain additional significance.

The Foundation is not simply waiting for something to happen.

People are being gathered.

Alexander knows the larger purpose, while Aleister appears to be one of the people responsible for making the necessary preparations happen.

His exact understanding of the Foundation's supernatural beliefs remains unclear.

That ambiguity is important because Aleister could be someone who:

• fully understands what is happening,
• believes without understanding,
• or simply follows orders because he trusts Alexander.

The story has not answered that yet.



Trivia

  • Aleister's most significant line so far is “I've gathered the unclean.”
  • His role contrasts with Alexander's: Alexander explains the philosophy; Aleister appears to execute the work.
  • He is one of the clearest examples of the Foundation functioning as an actual organization rather than merely a religious gathering.
  • His calmness makes the phrase “the unclean” more unsettling because he treats it as an ordinary task.
  • Unlike Alexander, Aleister hasn't explained his personal philosophy yet.
  • His lack of personal explanation makes him more mysterious than characters who openly reveal their beliefs.
  • He appears to operate comfortably within the Foundation's hierarchy.
  • The phrase “the unclean” raises an important unanswered question: who exactly qualifies as unclean?
  • His character currently works best as a Foundation operative, rather than as another version of Alexander.
  • Alexander is concerned with cultivation, faith, prophecy, and the Chosen One. Aleister's role appears much more practical: find the people, gather them, and prepare the ground.
  • His true personal loyalty has not yet been tested. It is currently unclear whether Aleister follows Alexander because of faith, family, fear, duty, or personal conviction.
