Basic Profile

Age : 18

Affiliation : Sterlinford College (student)

Status : Alive

Role : Protagonist; observer of Havenfall and its increasingly unnatural reality



Personality

Traits

  • Highly observant; notices small behavioral, environmental, and social details that other people overlook.
  • Emotionally detached; often recognizes what he is supposed to feel without actually experiencing the expected reaction.
  • Curious about people, especially when their behavior doesn't fit his understanding of them.
  • Analytical; instinctively breaks situations down into patterns, routines, and systems.
  • Comfortable with silence and solitude; often separates himself from crowds and watches rather than participates.
  • Socially capable when he chooses to be; he can deliberately adjust his behavior to make people feel safer.
  • Possesses a dry, sometimes unsettling sense of humor.
  • Patient with distressed people; he doesn't immediately try to fix them and can sit comfortably in silence.
  • Intensely curious about the strange; supernatural events tend to make him investigate rather than simply panic.
  • Has a strong need to understand what is happening, even when investigating puts him in danger.
  • Finds certain people and places inexplicably familiar, even when he cannot explain why.
  • Capable of genuine tenderness, particularly around Elizabeth, where his usual detachment becomes noticeably quieter.


Flaws

  • Can become so focused on observing that he fails to participate in his own life.
  • His emotional detachment can make disturbing situations feel like interesting information rather than emergencies.
  • Curiosity repeatedly overrides self-preservation.
  • Often rationalizes strange events instead of immediately accepting that something is seriously wrong.
  • Can become fixated on patterns and unanswered questions.
  • Has difficulty distinguishing between something being familiar and something being safe.
  • Sometimes treats people as subjects of observation rather than people he is emotionally connected to.
  • His willingness to investigate dangerous places can drag Elizabeth into situations she never wanted to enter.
  • His tendency to analyze his own reactions doesn't necessarily stop him from acting on them.
  • He can recognize that something is abnormal without feeling sufficiently concerned about it.


Motivations

  • Understanding the strange events occurring throughout Havenfall.
  • Understanding why he reacts differently from everyone around him.
  • Discovering the truth behind the Hole of Havenfall and the things emerging from it.
  • Understanding the connection between the Hole, the Edenus Foundation, Anthony, Martin, and the other unexplained events.
  • Finding meaning in the things he observes.
  • Remaining close to Elizabeth.
  • Determining whether Havenfall itself is what it appears to be.


Hates

  • Being forced into ordinary social expectations.
  • Being unable to understand something.
  • Being surrounded by people who blindly accept strange things as normal.
  • Losing control of his ability to observe and understand a situation.
  • Being unable to explain gaps in his own memory or perception.
  • Needless social noise and forced interaction.
  • Situations where the rules suddenly change without explanation.


Strengths

  • Exceptional observation skills.
  • Strong pattern recognition.
  • Can notice inconsistencies that others overlook.
  • Good at reading body language and adjusting his own behavior accordingly.
  • Comfortable approaching distressed people without immediately overwhelming them.
  • Remains unusually calm during dangerous or disturbing situations.
  • Willing to question things other people simply accept.
  • Has considerable patience when pursuing an unanswered question.
  • Can separate observation from immediate emotional reaction.
  • His curiosity allows him to notice connections between apparently unrelated events.


Fears

  • Not necessarily fear in the conventional sense, but the story suggests several deeper concerns:
  • Discovering that his understanding of reality is fundamentally wrong.
  • Being unable to determine what is real.
  • Losing Elizabeth.
  • Being unable to understand the people and systems surrounding him.
  • Discovering that the strange things happening around him are connected to him personally.
  • Becoming nothing more than an observer who watches life happen without truly belonging to it.
  • The possibility that Havenfall's people and institutions are not what he believes them to be.


Relationships

  • Elizabeth — The person around whom Cyrus becomes noticeably more emotionally present. Her presence gives him a rare sense of peace and companionship. She also acts as a social and practical counterweight to his investigative impulses.
  • Nova Zahwane — Classmate and friend. Nova's energetic personality contrasts sharply with Cyrus's detached nature. He becomes interested in her because she is new to Havenfall and therefore provides an outsider's perspective.
  • Billie Kagon — Someone Cyrus initially approaches while she is distressed. Their interaction reveals that his emotional detachment does not prevent him from understanding another person's discomfort.
  • Anthony Matthews — A figure Cyrus observes repeatedly and gradually becomes suspicious of. Anthony appears helpful and organized, but his connection to the Hole, Emily, Martin, and the Edenus Foundation makes him increasingly difficult for Cyrus to understand.
  • Emily — Cyrus becomes an observer of her strange connection to the Hole and the mysterious figure identified as “H.” His discovery of the letters and his observation of Anthony deepen his suspicion that something is manipulating her.
  • Martin Mathens — Initially connected to the death that follows Billie's betrayal, but later becomes important to Cyrus's understanding of Havenfall's hidden structure through the discoveries surrounding his investigation.
  • Havenfall — Almost a relationship in itself. Cyrus constantly watches the city as though it were a living system whose rules everyone else has forgotten to question.


Hobbies

  • Observing people and their behavior.
  • Walking through Havenfall alone.
  • Sitting on the Hill of Edenus.
  • Listening to music.
  • Watching the sky and surrounding environment.
  • Thinking about unusual information and unexplained events.
  • Spending quiet time with Elizabeth.
  • Investigating places and phenomena that other people would rather ignore.


Backstory

Cyrus Karmen is an 18-year-old student at Sterlinford College who has always seemed slightly out of step with the people around him. Rather than immediately experiencing situations emotionally, he tends to observe them first: how people move, how crowds react, how routines change, how someone speaks, and what they do when they believe nobody is watching.

His detachment becomes increasingly apparent when he experiences violent thoughts, emotional betrayal, and even death without producing the reactions expected of him. He recognizes the difference between his reactions and those of other people, but the realization rarely troubles him for long.

His relationship with Billie briefly gives him a form of connection, only for her betrayal to leave him emotionally displaced. Around the same period, Havenfall begins revealing stranger patterns: Martin Mathens's death, the Hole of Havenfall, people disappearing, the city entering its strange nightly state, and the appearance of Vario from the Hole.

Cyrus gradually becomes less interested in simply understanding individual people and more interested in understanding Havenfall itself.

His encounters with Elizabeth introduce another side of him. Around her, his constant observation becomes quieter. He describes the feeling of being with her as peaceful and eventually tells her that he wants her to stay forever. Yet even while experiencing this connection, his curiosity continues pulling him toward increasingly dangerous discoveries.

By the end of the eighth chapter, Cyrus has begun connecting Anthony to Martin's death and discovers evidence suggesting that Anthony's involvement extends far beyond a single murder. His investigation has also brought him dangerously close to the Edenus Foundation, the Hole, and whatever larger mechanism appears to be operating underneath Havenfall.



Trivia

Cyrus repeatedly observes people as though they are components of a larger system, describing students changing behavior when the college siren rings and the city becoming “contained” at night.

He also repeats “I am Cyrus Karmen. Today is 8 March 1997.” as a way of grounding himself, suggesting that maintaining his sense of identity and time may be important to him.

One of his strangest traits is that he can recognize something as abnormal without necessarily being frightened by it. Seeing Martin's corpse, for example, produces an unexpected sense of clarity rather than the horror he believes he should feel.

His relationship with Elizabeth is therefore especially significant: she is one of the few people who makes Cyrus stop observing the world long enough to actually experience being in it.
