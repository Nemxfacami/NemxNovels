Morning, 29 July 2020

She woke up in her bed, which she didn't question because her mother might have been the one who put her there.

"It's a Saturday," she said to herself, remembering that she had to go to extraclasses.

She got ready, took her lunchbox, and left.

On Saturdays, the school bus doesn't pick up students, so she had to walk to school.

Lusion was waiting for her outside.

Elizabeth thought maybe he was angry or disappointed—she was panicking but didn't show it.

Lusion was standing there as if he was about to kill her on the spot. But then he greeted her.

"Hey, Elizabeth." She greeted back, "Hey," unable to look him in the eyes.

"I can't take a no for this," he said, holding her hand.

"I want to take you out on a date."He smiled.

Morning, 27 July 2020



An alarm dragged her out of sleep, grating and relentless.

She groaned, sat up, and looked around. Strangely, her room was clean, though she remembered it being messy and untidy.

She didn't have time to question it. A shower, warm water cascading over her—heavenly, brief—and then she was in the kitchen, the house eerily quiet, as if no one else was home.

She blinked. Her mother was already there, snapping her fingers in front of her face.

"You're late for school!" A lunchbox pushed into her hands. Her little brother hurried past, though oddly, she couldn't see his face. She followed him out to where the school bus waited.

The bus took them to Arcus Academy, a massive institution buzzing with students.

The sheer presence of so many people overwhelmed her. Their loud, careless conversations irritated her.

"I heard Tiffany has a sex tape." "Last week was a bomb, we should do it again!"

The words floated around her like noise from a chaotic radio. She blinked, and she was already in class, the teacher not yet there, the room still noisy.

"Hey," a voice said next to her. It was Lusion—she hadn't even noticed him sitting beside her. His presence was calming, and suddenly the noise seemed to fade.

"Are you okay today? You seem... irritated," Lusion asked, leaning a little closer.

She gave a faint smile, though her expression remained cold. "Just annoyed by the noise."

"Ahh, yeah, the noise," Lusion repeated, nodding.

"It bores me too. For a second, I almost called the math teacher to come shut everyone up," he said proudly.

Elizabeth wasn't amused. She definitely didn't want the teacher to come in.

We all know how that ends. Still, Lusion seemed fun to be around.

"If you don't mind, meet me at the school gate after school," he said, standing, winking at the math teacher who'd just entered the room.

Elizabeth sighed.

The class was about to begin, and she was already exhausted.

At lunch, she left the classroom and headed for her favorite spot—the biggest tree on campus, her sanctuary. But today it wasn't the haven she hoped for. A boy was already there, sitting quietly in her spot.

What a disgusting scene, she thought.

"Hey, you... this is my spot," she said, her voice full of dominance, expecting him to move.

He looked startled, shy, almost scared.

"I... I'm sorry. I didn't have anywhere to sit in the cafeteria, and I'm new," he said nervously.

She noticed he was sweating.

He awkwardly offered his hand.

"I'm Chris Evans," he said with a forced smile.

Elizabeth saw through it but shook his sweaty hand anyway.

"I'm Elizabeth Cross." She sat a little farther away, keeping her distance.

"Just don't bring anyone here," she said, eyeing him from the corner of her eye.

She noticed he was sketching a woman in his notebook.

He hoped it wasn't sex.








At the Park

Lusion was sitting on a bench with Elizabeth, both eating ice cream.

She seemed to be enjoying herself, and this pleased Lusion.

It made him happy.

Lusion continued, "I'd take over the world for you and make a palace in the center of the world, where you'll look down on everyone while your innocence is bestowed upon them."

Elizabeth wasn't sure if he was joking or pretending to be a movie character, but she nodded as Lusion continued, "Everyone will be under you... you'll be at peace, a goddess."

"What if you destroy the world in the process?" Elizabeth asked him, looking at him with suspicion.

This question made Lusion flinch.

Simultaneously, a patrol truck crashed into a car nearby the park, and the explosion was huge enough to burn some of the trees.

Lusion protected Elizabeth with his own body, but luckily the fire didn't reach them.

They left the park and walked back to her home.

"I hope it wasn't a bad date," he said, looking at her.

This made Lusion smile.

"I'm glad... this is where I leave," he said, getting close to her—close enough that their noses were nearly touching.

He leaned in further and whispered in her ear,"Your happiness brings light into a burnt world, and keeps this one alive."

Then he left, leaving Elizabeth confused.

When she entered in the house, she found her mother in the kitchen, stressed, even crying while holding her phone.

When she saw Elizabeth enter, she was happy to see her—she even went to hug her.

"I thought you might have gotten hurt by the explosion at school," her mom said, looking Elizabeth in the eyes and even touching her face as she smiled.
