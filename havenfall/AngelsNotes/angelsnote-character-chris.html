In truth he has been watching her for far longer, keeping a hideout, a wall of photos of everyone who might love her, and a plan he calls “the plan.” His familiarity with her room, her scent, and her nickname hints at a shared past █████ ████ █████. Among his rivals, he declares Ted and Sam “out,” leaving Tom, Elliott, Lusion, and Botas still on the wall.
