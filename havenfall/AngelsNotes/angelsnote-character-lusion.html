Personality

Traits

  • Charming, calm, and attentive; makes Elizabeth feel safe.
  • Possessive beneath the warmth (“you’ll always be mine”).
  • Claims to lack emotions, yet feels intensely about Elizabeth.
  • Violent with precision; his anger is quick, controlled, and silent.
  • Persuasive and philosophical; speaks of worlds, palaces, and goddesses.
  • Manipulates through guilt, comfort, and carefully chosen words.
  • Patient; waits for the right moment.


Flaws

  • Treats Elizabeth as something to protect and own
  • Isolates her by undermining other relationships
  • Cold toward people he sees as threats
  • Hides the truth behind kindness


Motivations

  • Keeping Elizabeth beside him, protected and “his alone”
  • Building a world where she is at peace, even at a cost
  • Preventing others, especially Botas, from taking her


Hates

  • Botas and what he represents
  • Being told no or being questioned (“I didn’t ask you”)
  • Anything that threatens Elizabeth
  • Losing her to the past


Strengths

  • Calm under pressure
  • Knows how to comfort and persuade
  • A deep, long history with Elizabeth


Fears

  • Losing Elizabeth
  • Her remembering what he did
  • Her choosing someone else


Relationships

  • Botas — his rival, whom he permits “for now”

Hobbies

Backstory

Lusion is the first person Elizabeth sees after waking on the Hill of Edenus, and he has been at her side ever since. His history with her goes back to childhood, when he wrote her a letter in fifth grade promising he would marry her and received a bracelet in return. But another memory resurfaces: a dark room, a razor, and his calm voice telling her no one can hear her █████ ████ █████. He insists his goal is her happiness, whatever it takes.



Trivia
