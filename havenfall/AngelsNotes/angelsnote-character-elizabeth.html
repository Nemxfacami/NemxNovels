Role : Protagonist; the girl who lost her memories



Personality

Traits

  • Cold and guarded with strangers, but warm underneath.
  • Deeply empathetic; wants to understand people before judging them, even those who frighten her.
  • Curious about danger and about the people who threaten her.
  • Easily overwhelmed by noise and crowds; seeks quiet, solitary places such as the biggest tree on campus.
  • Bonds quickly with whoever makes her feel safe in the moment.
  • Reflective; processes her confusion through her diary and long silences.
  • Resilient; recovers her composure after shock and grief, though slowly.


Flaws

  • Mistakes familiarity and nostalgia for safety
  • Lets others define her worth
  • Struggles to say no or resist when frightened or frozen
  • Drawn to unhealthy attachment because she fears being alone


Motivations

  • Recovering her lost memories
  • Understanding who she was and who the people around her really are
  • Feeling safe and truly connected to someone


Hates

  • Noise and careless crowds
  • Being helpless
  • Having her space invaded
  • Not knowing the truth about her own past


Strengths

  • Emotional insight and empathy
  • Honesty with herself, even when it hurts (“maybe deep down I wanted to be with him”)
  • Ability to push back at critical moments
  • Quiet persistence in piecing together her past


Fears

  • Being alone
  • Being only a “toy” or background character in her own story
  • Losing the people she loves
  • Never getting her memories back


Relationships

  • Lusion — her savior in the present and her comfort, with a hidden and darker history she cannot remember
  • Chris Evans — her stalker; frightening, yet she is strangely drawn to understanding him
  • Botas — her boyfriend of over a year; safe, familiar, and slowly returning to her memory
  • Mrs. Cross — her mother, whose death in Chapter 5 shattered her world


Hobbies

  • Writing in her diary, addressed to “Lizza”
  • Sitting beneath the great tree on the Hill of Edenus and the one on campus
  • Walking through the city with the people she trusts


Backstory

Elizabeth first appears lying beneath a great tree on the Hill of Edenus, with no memory of who she is. Lusion walks her home, and from there she follows what she is told: a school life at Arcus Academy, a mother, a little brother whose face she cannot see, and a boyfriend she does not remember having. Her past returns only in fragments: a first date with Botas, a childhood letter and bracelet from Lusion, and a dark room that she has hidden even from herself. Marks on her arms suggest those memories are not as harmless as they seem █████ ████ █████.



Trivia

Her diary entries are addressed to “Lizza,” the nickname Botas uses. Every date with Lusion feels strangely nostalgic, “exactly the same way” as before.
