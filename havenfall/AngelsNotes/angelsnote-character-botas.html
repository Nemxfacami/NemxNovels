Backstory

Botas asked Elizabeth to be his girlfriend beneath the tree on the Hill of Edenus, a spot he calls “the beginning.” He comes from a wealthy family in a large mansion, where cheerful broadcasts praise the Angel System and everyone repeats gentle, rehearsed lines. While the rest of his family relaxes, he alone feels something is wrong, and his mother’s reassurances only deepen his unease.
