THE EDENUS BANK ROBBERY THAT STILL HAS PEOPLE TALKING

Havenfall Chronicles
Share:

EDENUS BANK ROBBERY

HAVENFALL — It has been years since the Edenus District bank robbery, but some residents still remember the strange series of events that followed.

The robbery occurred shortly after midday, when two masked individuals entered the bank and demanded money from the tellers.

Police arrived minutes later.

The suspects had already escaped.

According to investigators, the pair fled the building carrying several bags of cash before disappearing into the surrounding streets.

What happened next, however, turned the robbery into one of Edenus' strangest local stories.

One of the getaway vehicles was discovered abandoned less than a mile from the bank.

The vehicle was unlocked.

The engine was still running.

And the money was gone.

For several hours, police searched the surrounding area without finding the suspects.

Then, late that afternoon, a local gardener discovered several bundles of cash hidden inside a large public trash container.

More money was found scattered across a nearby alley.

Authorities eventually recovered a significant portion of the stolen cash, but the suspects were never publicly identified.

The case remains officially listed as unsolved.

What makes the robbery particularly memorable to Edenus residents is not simply the amount of money stolen, but how poorly the escape appeared to have gone.

"I remember everyone talking about it," one longtime resident said. "It was the strangest robbery we'd ever had."

Even years later, theories about what happened continue to circulate around Edenus.

Some believe the criminals panicked.

Others believe the money was deliberately abandoned.

And a few residents insist there was a third person involved who was never caught.

Police have never confirmed any of those theories.

The Edenus bank has since undergone several renovations.

The old building is gone.

The robbery, however, hasn't been forgotten.
