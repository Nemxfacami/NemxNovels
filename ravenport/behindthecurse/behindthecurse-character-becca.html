Backstory

Very little is established about Becca's childhood or personal life, but her position within the Beaumont family is clear.

She belongs to a family whose bloodline is governed by an ancient curse. The Beaumonts have survived for generations by following a complicated collection of traditions and rules.

Becca understands that the curse is not simply a supernatural entity attacking the family. She explains that the curse itself is a rule, while the spirit associated with it acts as an enforcer of that rule.

The curse is tied to the continuation of the Beaumont bloodline. Family names, traditions, marriages, burial practices, and the creation of new generations all contribute to maintaining the system.

Becca also explains that outsiders can become part of the family through traditional marriage ceremonies. Once accepted through the proper ceremony, they become subject to the same family system.

One of the most important things she teaches Bella is that the curse does not necessarily punish love directly. Instead, the person a Beaumont loves may become the target.

Becca also explains that nobody has successfully defeated the curse. The Beaumonts have instead survived by obeying its rules and learning how to live around them. Some family members have attempted to reject the system, only to disappear or die.

The family's naming tradition is another part of this inherited structure. Beaumont children are generally given names beginning with B, although the original reason for the tradition is no longer known.

Raker Beaumont was one of the notable exceptions. He refused to change his name and became increasingly interested in discovering the origin of the curse. Becca knows that Raker eventually found something, but she refuses to reveal the discovery to Bella.

Eventually, Beatrice chooses Becca to become Bella's teacher.

This decision places Becca directly between Bella and Bianca. Bianca dislikes Becca and insists that she would be a better teacher, but Becca is given the responsibility anyway.

Her role is therefore not simply to frighten Bella with stories about the family. She is preparing Bella to understand the system she was born into before the curse fully becomes her responsibility.
