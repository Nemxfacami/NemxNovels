Relationships

  • Bella Beaumont — Bella is Lucas's most important relationship in the first ten chapters.

    Their relationship begins in an unusual way.

    Bella hears Lucas's voice inside her mind before she ever meets him physically.

    At first, their communication is simply strange. Eventually, however, it becomes companionship.

    Bella talks to Lucas about her problems, her family, her emotions, and ordinary parts of her life. Lucas becomes someone she can speak to without having to perform a particular version of herself.

    Their first physical meeting occurs at Oakwood Public Library.

    Bella immediately recognizes Lucas from his voice.

    The meeting is initially awkward because they have already developed a relationship without ever seeing each other.

    That awkwardness quickly disappears.

    They walk together, joke, eat, discuss university, and spend hours simply talking.

    Lucas also explains something important about himself to Bella: when he is emotionally struggling, he doesn't always want someone to immediately try to fix him. Sometimes he prefers strange questions or dark conversations because they redirect his attention.

    Bella listens and begins understanding how he works.

    Their relationship gradually becomes affectionate.

    Bella admits that she likes looking at him and eventually tells him that she wants to stay with him.

    Lucas also makes it clear that having Bella beside him feels better than simply hearing her voice in his head.

    By Chapter 10, Bella has become the person Lucas trusts enough to remain beside him even after discovering that her own family is attempting to perform a ritual on him.

    Their relationship ultimately becomes a choice against the Beaumont family's control.
  • Damon — Damon is one of Lucas's recurring university connections.

    Their relationship begins when Damon approaches Lucas while looking for a room and later asks Lucas for help finding a mysterious door.

    Lucas accompanies Damon around the university despite having his own things going on.

    Damon's strange behavior makes him one of the recurring mysteries surrounding Lucas's university life.

    Their friendship is unconventional, with both characters frequently wandering into strange situations without behaving as though they are particularly unusual.
  • Gwen — Gwen is a gothic student Lucas encounters at the university.

    Their first interaction begins with Gwen asking about a room.

    Lucas gives her information and offers to spend several hours talking with her.

    She declines because she has other plans.

    Later, they have a more meaningful conversation beneath a tree.

    Gwen talks about people saying negative things about her, while Lucas responds with his characteristic blunt humor.

    Their conversation eventually turns toward life, loneliness, and having a colorful mind despite darker thoughts.

    Gwen provides another example of Lucas forming connections through unusual conversations rather than conventional social interaction.
  • Jay — Jay is one of Lucas's acquaintances.

    Jay visits Lucas's apartment and becomes involved in Lucas's everyday life.

    Lucas allows Jay to stay at his apartment after Jay brings Damon home.

    Their relationship is casual and humorous, with Jay becoming part of the strange collection of people who pass through Lucas's life.
  • Kim — Kim is another university connection.

    Lucas helps her find her psychology class after she has difficulty locating the correct room.

    Their interactions are relatively casual, but Kim becomes part of Lucas's expanding university social circle.
  • Valerie — Valerie works at the library and becomes one of the recurring university characters Lucas encounters.

    She is present during several events involving Lucas and Damon.

    Her strange reactions and interactions with other characters also contribute to the sense that Lucas's university environment contains more mysteries than it initially appears to.
  • Bella's Family — Lucas initially knows Bella primarily through her own descriptions of her family.

    This changes dramatically when he visits the Beaumont house.

    Benjamin and Bianca initially treat him as a guest.

    But the family eventually drugs him and takes him to their family house.

    Lucas becomes the target of the Beaumont ritual because of his connection to Bella.

    The encounter turns Lucas from an outsider observing strange events into a direct participant in the Beaumont curse.

Backstory

Lucas Asterisk enters the story as an unusual university student whose life appears relatively ordinary until his interactions with Ravenport's stranger inhabitants begin accumulating.

He is associated with Oakwood University and spends much of his time wandering between classes, rooms, students, libraries, and strange locations.

Unlike many people around him, Lucas does not seem particularly disturbed by the bizarre things he encounters.

He often reacts to strange circumstances with curiosity.

At one point he enters a classroom where something feels wrong with the university itself.

The classrooms appear empty despite the university being populated.

Even the clock seems to behave strangely.

Lucas also encounters a mysterious ritual involving blood, bodies, an old woman, and a teddy bear.

Rather than abandoning his curiosity, he continues exploring.

His encounters eventually lead him to Cavewoods.

There he encounters a fisherman beneath a cliff and later meets Damon, who claims to be looking for a room.

Lucas's life continues to blur the line between ordinary university life and unexplained supernatural events.

At the same time, Lucas develops a reputation for knowing things.

He sells information about university rooms and locations to people who want it.

This makes him something of an informal information broker.

He is willing to trade information for money or conversation, depending on what interests him.

Lucas's philosophical side becomes increasingly apparent as he walks through Ravenport alone.

He begins thinking about the structure of human life:

school leads to knowledge, knowledge leads to work, work leads to money, money leads to survival, and survival eventually leads to death.

To him, civilization can resemble a complicated zoo.

He imagines another kind of existence instead.

A small house.

A forest.

Nature.

Quiet.

Peace.

He would rather live a shorter life that genuinely feels like living than spend a long life following expectations without ever feeling alive.

This reveals something fundamental about Lucas.

His problem is not simply that he is unhappy.

He struggles with the idea of what makes life worth continuing in the first place.

When he is emotionally exhausted, he sometimes thinks about disappearing.

He explicitly clarifies that this does not mean he intends to kill himself.

Instead, the thought appears when he becomes overwhelmed by life and expectations.

His preferred response is often distraction.

Music occupies most of his waking time when he is not feeling okay.

He also works until he becomes too tired to continue thinking.

He eventually explains this to Bella.

Rather than wanting someone to immediately solve his emotional problems, he sometimes prefers dark questions, strange subjects, or unusual conversations.

This becomes one of the foundations of their relationship.

Lucas's connection with Bella begins before they ever meet.

Bella hears his voice in her mind.

Neither of them initially understands exactly why.

Despite the supernatural nature of the connection, it gradually becomes comfortable.

Bella becomes someone Lucas can talk to.

When they finally meet at Oakwood Public Library, the relationship changes.

The voice now has a face.

They begin spending time together physically.

They walk around Ravenport.

They eat together.

They joke.

They talk about university.

They discuss emotions.

They spend hours together.

Lucas gradually becomes more comfortable around Bella.

Eventually, the two return toward the university and agree to do it again.

For Lucas, this relationship represents something he has been searching for throughout the story without explicitly naming it:

someone whose presence makes life feel less empty.

Then the Beaumont family enters Lucas's life directly.

Bella invites him to her home.

The family appears to treat him as a normal guest.

Benjamin introduces himself.

Bianca prepares dinner.

Lucas sits with the family.

But Bella senses that something is wrong.

She warns him telepathically.

They consider escaping.

Before they can act, Lucas suddenly becomes ill.

He collapses.

He wakes inside the Beaumont family's ritual.

Eight members of the family surround him.

Candles fill the basement.

An ancient ritual is performed.

Something appears behind Lucas.

A tall shadow enters his body.

Yet Lucas remains himself.

The ritual fails.

When Bianca asks who he is, Lucas simply answers:

"I'm Lucas Asterisk."

The Beaumont family reacts with panic.

They had expected something to happen to him.

Instead, Lucas remains Lucas.

The family discusses Baldwin, an ancestor who apparently had some connection to what happened.

Bella notices Lucas's shadow behaving strangely.

Then the basement erupts into supernatural chaos.

A shadow appears.

Bianca identifies it as Grandpa Raker.

The lights go out.

The candles extinguish.

The temperature drops.

The family loses control.

Bella and Lucas escape.

They run into the woods together.

Lucas admits that he is scared.

Bella admits that she is scared too.

He takes her hand.

Bella tells him she wants to stay with him.

They continue running.

By the end of Chapter 10, Lucas has gone from a university student who spends his days wandering around Ravenport to someone directly entangled with one of the city's oldest supernatural bloodlines.

And the ritual raises a major unanswered question:

Why did the Beaumont family's ritual fail on Lucas?



Trivia

  • Lucas's surname is Asterisk, making his name unusually distinctive even within the strange world of Ravenport.
  • He has a strong interest in fishing and is associated with the nickname "Fisherman."
  • He has a tendency to wander rather than follow a rigid plan.
  • He is comfortable selling information to people who need it.
  • He once sells information about a university room rather than simply giving it away.
  • Lucas has a strange ability to remain calm around bizarre circumstances.
  • His philosophical thinking frequently turns ordinary experiences into questions about existence.
  • His ideal life involves nature, isolation, quiet, and peace rather than conventional success.
  • He views the normal progression of education → work → money → survival → death as an almost mechanical system.
  • Lucas's emotional coping mechanism is largely based around distraction, exhaustion, music, and unusual conversation.
  • Music occupies roughly 95% of his waking time when he is emotionally unwell.
  • He doesn't necessarily want people to solve his problems when he is struggling.
  • He prefers being redirected toward strange or dark subjects until he is emotionally capable of dealing with the original problem.
  • His first relationship with Bella exists entirely through a voice before they ever physically meet.
  • Bella recognizes Lucas by his voice before she fully processes him as a person standing in front of her.
  • Lucas is one of the few characters who can interact with Bella without immediately trying to control or instruct her.
  • His connection to Bella eventually places him directly in the path of the Beaumont curse.
  • The Beaumont family expected their ritual to change him, but it apparently failed.
  • Whatever entered Lucas during the ritual did not produce the transformation the Beaumont family expected.
  • His answer to Bianca's question — "I'm Lucas Asterisk" — is significant because his identity appears to remain intact.
  • Bella notices that Lucas's shadow moves one second later than he does after the ritual.
  • Raker's appearance during the ritual suggests that Lucas may have a connection to the Beaumont family's past that neither Lucas nor Bella understands.
  • Lucas's greatest strength may also be his greatest mystery: he treats the impossible as something worth investigating rather than something worth immediately fearing.
  • His story repeatedly places him between two worlds: ordinary university life and the supernatural world beneath Ravenport.
  • By the end of Chapter 10, Lucas is no longer simply investigating strange things in Ravenport.
  • Something strange has begun happening to him.
