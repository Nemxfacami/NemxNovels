Personality

Traits

  • Greedy: Travis is heavily motivated by money and immediately prioritizes payment when dealing with the Black Templars.
  • Confident: He behaves as though he is always in control, even when dealing with supernatural objects.
  • Fearless: Travis shows remarkably little fear when confronted with the strange coffin.
  • Pragmatic: He is primarily concerned with what can be gained from a situation.
  • Boss-like: Travis naturally takes charge of the group and makes decisions for the others.
  • Absurd: His behavior can become almost ridiculous, particularly when he treats a goat as his horse.
  • Opportunistic: He sees valuable discoveries as opportunities to make money.
  • Persistent: He continues with the coffin deal even after discovering that something is clearly wrong.
  • Blunt: Travis speaks directly and does not spend much time worrying about appearances.
  • Irreverent: He treats situations that should be terrifying with an almost inappropriate level of casualness.


Flaws

  • Greedy: Money can override his better judgment.
  • Overconfident: Travis underestimates how dangerous the coffin is.
  • Reckless: He is willing to transport an unknown supernatural object for profit.
  • Morally indifferent: Grave robbing, kidnapping, and criminal activity do not appear to trouble him.
  • Dismissive of danger: He refuses to take the coffin's supernatural warning signs seriously at first.
  • Impulsive: His decisions are often driven by immediate opportunities.
  • Poor risk assessment: He continues negotiating around the coffin instead of treating it as a serious threat.
  • Treats people as assets: Jason is kidnapped and pressured into helping because Travis needs another person for the operation.


Motivations

Fears

Backstory

Travis Dell Deyvon is a thirty-five-year-old criminal operating with a small group of associates.

His background before the events of the story is not established, but his behavior shows that grave robbery and criminal dealing are not completely unfamiliar activities to him.

Travis travels into the mountains of western Ravenport with Thomas, Fletcher, and Alan after hearing about a grave near the Mount Twin sanctuary.

The grave is rumored to contain something worth five million crowns.

When they locate it, they dig up a coffin bearing strange carvings.

The discovery immediately becomes more complicated when Jason refuses to help them. Travis's group kidnaps him and forces him into the operation, eventually agreeing to give him a percentage of the money.

Jayden later arrives and nearly crashes while attempting to reach them.

The group manages to load the coffin and takes it away.

The coffin soon proves to be unusual.

Thomas hears knocking coming from inside it and becomes sufficiently frightened to nail the coffin shut.

Rather than abandoning it, Travis continues pursuing the potential profit.

He eventually arranges a meeting with the Black Templars.

While waiting for them, Travis sits on a goat and casually calls it his horse. He even tells an absurd story about horses and goats supposedly once being unicorns.

The scene demonstrates Travis's strange sense of humor and his ability to behave casually even when surrounded by increasingly bizarre circumstances.

When the Black Templars arrive, they bring enormous amounts of money, offering ten million crowns each.

Travis immediately focuses on the payment.

The Templars inspect the coffin and become increasingly concerned about the chains and nails covering it.

They order the nails to be removed.

Travis refuses.

His attitude is essentially that they can take the coffin or leave it, but he is not going to lose the deal.

The Templars attempt to move the coffin anyway.

It falls and cracks.

The temperature suddenly drops, and everyone's breath becomes visible.

The Templars recognize that the coffin is unholy.

They immediately order everyone into the car and abandon the coffin.

Travis's greed nearly causes him and his crew to remain in the area with something they clearly do not understand.

The Black Templars leave the payment behind, meaning Travis technically gets the money he wanted despite losing the coffin.

The coffin, however, is not finished.

The nails near the crack begin lifting themselves.

Whatever is inside has not been contained.

Travis therefore becomes one of the first characters in the story to accidentally interact with a supernatural threat through greed rather than curiosity.

He did not seek the coffin because he wanted to understand it.

He wanted to sell it.
