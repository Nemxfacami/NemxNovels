Backstory

Bennett's childhood and early life are not established in detail.

He was born into the Beaumont family and therefore grew up within the same inherited system of traditions and supernatural rules affecting the rest of the family.

Unlike Bella, whose understanding of the curse develops during the story, Bennett appears to already exist within the family's established environment.

His early appearances make the Beaumont household look relatively ordinary. He has relationships, watches movies, interacts with family members, and gets into the kinds of trouble that might occur in any family.

One of the clearest examples is his relationship with Chelsea.

Bennett is caught with Chelsea by Benjamin, creating a confrontation inside the Beaumont household. The incident establishes Bennett as someone who is willing to make personal choices even when those choices may conflict with family expectations.

His appearance also changes dramatically later in the story.

Bella sees Bennett after he has become bald, with tattoos and scriptures covering his head. The tattoos are connected to the Black Templars, adding another unexplained element to Bennett's character and suggesting that his life may involve forces outside the Beaumont family.

Despite these strange developments, Bennett continues to exist within the family household.

Later, when Bella returns home, Bennett is absent. When he eventually returns, he casually discovers that Bella has a male friend, Lucas. His reaction shows that he had previously thought of Bella as something of a loner.

This interaction becomes important because Lucas's presence eventually brings the Beaumont family's supernatural plans into motion.

Bennett is therefore positioned in an interesting place within the family: he is not the person explaining the curse, nor is he the primary person controlling Bella. Instead, he is one of the younger family members living inside the same strange system while maintaining pieces of an apparently ordinary life.

His relationship with Chelsea, interest in horror, unusual tattoos, and connection to the Black Templars suggest that there is considerably more to Bennett's story than the chapters have currently revealed.
