Backstory

Raker Beaumont was born into the Beaumont family and lived under the same curse that has controlled generations of the bloodline.

However, Raker was different from many Beaumonts.

The family has a tradition of giving its children names beginning with the letter B.

Raker refused to follow it.

Rather than changing his identity to fit the family's expectations, he kept the name Raker.

His refusal was more than a naming disagreement. It represented his broader unwillingness to accept Beaumont traditions without questioning them.

At some point in his life, Raker began investigating the Beaumont curse itself.

The family had already developed a method of survival: obey the rules, preserve the bloodline, maintain the traditions, and avoid breaking the conditions imposed upon them.

Raker wanted something different.

He wanted to know where the curse came from.

According to Becca, Raker eventually found something during his investigation.

Exactly what he found remains unknown.

The family has preserved the fact that he discovered something without preserving — or revealing — the complete explanation of what it was.

Raker eventually died, but death did not completely separate him from the Beaumont family.

During the ritual performed on Lucas, something goes wrong.

After the ritual fails, the basement becomes violently supernatural. The candles extinguish, the temperature drops, the lights flicker, and a violent wind moves through the room.

A shadow appears.

Bianca recognizes it immediately.

“Grandpa Raker.”

She tells him that it is her.

The shadow pauses before pulling away, apparently recognizing Bianca.

The supernatural presence then disappears from her immediate vicinity before eventually moving across Benevide and entering him.

This establishes that Raker is not simply a name in Beaumont history.

Something connected to him remains capable of appearing within the family.

His exact relationship to the shadow is not fully explained. It is not established whether the shadow is literally Raker's spirit, an aspect of him, or something using his appearance.

What is established is that Bianca recognizes it as Raker and that the entity responds to her.

Raker's story therefore remains unfinished.

He spent his life attempting to understand the curse, discovered something important, and died without the full truth becoming known.

Yet his presence continues to appear when the Beaumont family's supernatural system begins to break down.
