"The Great Erasal."

Mayor Livius addressed the city yesterday under what was described as a “routine disclosure briefing.” No press syndicates carried it live. No official transcript exists. And yet, fragments of the broadcast persist—glitched recordings, partial captions, audio that cuts just before clarity.

But the message is consistent.

We forgot.

Not naturally. Not gradually. But all at once.

According to Livius, humanity did not evolve into its current state of confusion—we were *reduced* to it. Our history, our progress, our conflicts… removed. Not destroyed, but erased from memory. Cleanly. Systematically.

The cause?

An external force.

What he referred to only as “the Visitors.”

They did not arrive recently. That is the first misconception. They came long ago—long enough for their presence to become normal, invisible. They didn’t conquer through war, but through correction. They studied us, overcame us, and then did something far more permanent:

They rewrote us.

Not physically. Cognitively.

Memories gone. Context gone. Identity fragmented.

And in that silence, they remained.

Hidden in plain sight.

Positions of power. Systems of control. Structures we trust without question.

Governments. Institutions. Even cities like Ravenport itself.

Livius did not claim innocence.

In fact, what unsettled viewers most was his final statement—repeated in every surviving fragment:

> “Exposure does not imply resistance.”

No one knows what he meant.

Was he revealing the truth… or managing it?

Because if the Great Erasal is real, then the question is no longer *what happened to us*—

It’s:

Who among us remembers… and why?

Since the broadcast, reports have surfaced worldwide. People describing sudden flashes—memories that don’t belong to this version of reality. Structures that feel familiar but unplaceable. Languages half-understood. Fear without origin.

And beneath it all, a growing suspicion:

We are not recovering the past.

We are *interfering with something that was meant to stay buried.*

As for Mayor Livius—

No confirmed sightings since the address.

No official statement.

Only speculation.

Some believe he is human… a fragment that somehow resisted the Erasal.

Others believe something far worse:

That he was never exposing the truth—

Only preparing us for it.
