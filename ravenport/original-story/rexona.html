Rexona bleeds because it is trying to simulate life, but failing.

It is an anomaly that:
- Absorbs biological patterns
- Attempts to mimic organic systems
- Cannot sustain internal structure

So instead of functioning like a body, it leaks its imitation of one → blood.

But that blood is not real blood.

It’s:

- chemically unstable
- partially synthetic
- partially remembered biology

A civilian-grade containment shell disguised as hygiene.

- The anomaly tries to become alive
- Uses the fuel as a medium
- Fails
- Expels corrupted residue → “blood”

That’s why it’s dangerous:

- It burns through bone
- It reacts unpredictably with organic tissue
- It spreads structural decay
