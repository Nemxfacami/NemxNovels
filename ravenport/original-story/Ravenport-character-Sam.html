Backstory

Born in a house of noise, her mother enjoys drowning herself in a bottle of alcohol, as do her friends and her boyfriend. A house full of zombie-walking humans who fire sharp blades out of their mouths at Samantha. The house knows only alcohol as food. The fridge starves; it looks like a skeleton. A narcissistic mother roams the house with her mighty self in thought that whatever she takes is hers. She enters her room and steals, and when Sam finds out she took whatever wasn't hers, she pretends, plays the victim. Blames Samantha for doing so in her room. She hides food and wastes money for booze. She punches her daughter and slaps her with hate, yet she birthed her. She curses the girl for being on this world and shows others great love. Shows disappointment in her, yet she scratches the walls of her inside to tear and shape herself in ways her mom wishes. She roars at her for not being good enough, not being the child that she had given birth to. Mother's love? Tears fall and fill her room. After every night of great wondrous parties of drunkards exchanging useless words, the House looks like downtown streets. She cleans the whole town for 6 hours. Has she eaten? No, for her stomach cries and she hears not. She drowns in darkness and no one sees. She lives a life that isn't hers. The razors and knives are her helper and comforter.
